# Summarize

Turn a long artifact into the few sentences that matter.

## Steps
1. Read the full source once — do not summarize from a summary.
2. Identify the audience and the decision the summary must support.
3. Extract the load-bearing facts and any open questions or risks.
4. Write 3–7 bullets (or a short paragraph), lead with the conclusion.
5. Preserve exact numbers, names, and dates; drop filler and restatement.

## Principles
- Accuracy over brevity; brevity over completeness.
- Never introduce a claim the source does not support.
- If the source is ambiguous, say so — do not paper over it.
